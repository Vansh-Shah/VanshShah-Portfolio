## — The Story

Six years leading teams under pressure. Two years keeping banking systems online for mutual banks and credit unions across Australia. Now building toward cybersecurity leadership — Master of Cybersecurity at UNSW, averaging 80%.

It didn't start in IT. Before any SQL query or incident ticket, I spent six years supervising the front end at Woolworths — managing teams, de-escalating difficult situations, and learning that calm under pressure is something you earn shift by shift. That foundation doesn't come from a textbook.

RMIT's Bachelor of Information Technology gave me the technical language to match those instincts, and opened the door into fintech. I joined Ultradata Australia in February 2024 as a Technical Support Consultant.

The Master of Cybersecurity at UNSW is the next step — moving from responding to incidents to preventing them. It runs alongside the day job rather than replacing it. The goal is senior security leadership: not just someone who can fix things, but someone who can build organisations that are resilient before things break.

## — Projects

### [SSL/TLS Certificate Monitor](https://github.com/Vansh-Shah/SSL-Monitor) · `Python · Security` · ![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-16A34A?style=flat)

Desktop GUI + CLI tool that checks SSL/TLS certificate expiry across multiple domains in parallel. Features a full domain manager, colour-coded results table, JSON/CSV export, and CI-friendly exit codes. Zero external dependencies.

### [IIS Log Viewer](https://github.com/Vansh-Shah/iis-log-viewer) · `JavaScript · Log Analysis` · ![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-16A34A?style=flat)

Single-file, fully client-side viewer for W3C IIS log files — filter, sort and search, drag-select a time window on a live histogram, and decode IIS substatus and Win32 codes. Nothing leaves the browser.

### UVDiag Lock Analysis · `UniVerse · Diagnostics` · ![Internal](https://img.shields.io/badge/Internal-FBBF24?style=flat)

Browser-based analyser for UniVerse uvdiag captures — reports blockers and deadlocks with a wait-for graph. Built for banking support work; source is internal to Ultradata.
